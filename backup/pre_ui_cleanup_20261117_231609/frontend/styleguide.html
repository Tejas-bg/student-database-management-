A university identity, carried with care

02 · Colour

University maroon first, gold as the signal

Warm ivory surfaces and maroon structure carry most of the interface. University gold marks active states, focus, key indicators, and the primary action in dark theme. Gold is never used as body text on white because it does not have enough contrast; it is a surface and accent colour. Status colours are muted and always paired with text or an icon.

03 · Typography

Inter for reading, JetBrains Mono for signals

A four-level hierarchy keeps screens calm: display, section heading, body, and technical label. Uppercase is limited to short technical labels with tracking so it never becomes hard to read.

04 · Spacing and shape

A 4px rhythm, restrained radii

Spacing is based on a 4px unit. Cards use 16px radii, controls use 10px, and pills are fully rounded. Shadows are subtle and used only for menus, dialogs, and toasts.

05 · Actions

Buttons and navigation items

One primary action per region. Secondary buttons carry supporting actions, ghost buttons stay quiet, and danger is reserved for destructive intent. Every button keeps a visible focus ring and a 40px minimum target height.

14 · Accessibility

Readable, keyboard-friendly, and honest

The GMU Management System targets WCAG 2.1 AA. Accessibility is treated as part of the visual design, not a cleanup step: focus is visible, targets are large enough, colour is paired with text, and motion respects the operating system.

  • Visible focus ring on every interactive element, with a gold halo and a solid outline that meets contrast in both themes.

  • Semantic landmarks: sidebar, header, main, footer, breadcrumb, and a skip link to main content.

  • Tabs use roving tabindex and arrow keys. Dialogs use native focus trapping. Menus close on Escape.

  • Charts include accessible labels and visible summaries; the performance chart also has a data-table alternative.

  • All animation is shortened to 0.01ms underprefers-reduced-motion: reduce, and skeleton shimmer stops.

Contrast verification

The measured contrast pairs and the verification method are recorded indocs/design/ACCESSIBILITY.md. If a pair fails, it is listed honestly rather than hidden.
